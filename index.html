We study how the brain and body work together to keep us upright and moving, and how that control changes with age, fatigue, neurodegeneration, and neurodiversity (such as autism and Down syndrome). Our goal is better ways to measure neuromotor function, and ultimately to support safe, independent movement across the lifespan.
