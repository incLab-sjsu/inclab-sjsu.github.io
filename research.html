Keeping upright and moving safely looks effortless, but it depends on a continuous conversation between the brain, the senses, and the muscles. The Intelligent Neuromotor Control Lab studies that conversation: how the nervous system and the muscles together produce posture, gait, and balance, and how that control changes with aging, fatigue, neurodegeneration, and neurodiversity.

Our work combines laboratory movement analysis with data-driven methods, with the long-term aim of practical tools that detect changes in neuromotor function early and support safe, independent mobility.
